Services

Everything you need to stand out and get hired.

One eight-session program that covers your resume, LinkedIn, job search, networking and interviews.

What we’ll work on together

The four areas the program covers, all in one plan.

  • Resume and LinkedIn Optimization

    We rewrite your resume to get past ATS, then fix the LinkedIn headline, summary and keywords recruiters search for.

  • Job Search Strategy

    We pick your target companies, shape your personal brand and research each role before you apply.

  • Networking

    You build real connections that open doors, with message templates and a weekly plan for reaching the people who hire.

  • Mock Interviews

    You practice with someone who ran real interviews and get feedback in real time.

The program

The eight-session program

Your schedule, your pace.

Take one session a week, a few sessions in one week, or even the whole program in a single day. You decide what works best for you. To get the best results, the program should be completed within two months.

Every session ends with real work done, not just advice.

  1. 01

    Session 1

    Your Starting Point

    We talk about your major, your experience, your goals and what has held your search back. You fill out a Career Interest Form so we know exactly where to focus.

    You leave with: a personal job search game plan and clear goals.

  2. 02

    Session 2

    Your Strengths and Your Story

    We uncover what makes you stand out, including class projects, part-time jobs, clubs and volunteering. Everything counts.

    You leave with: a 30-second pitch for career fairs and a confident answer to “Tell me about yourself.”

  3. 03

    Session 3

    Target Roles and Companies

    We match your strengths to roles that fit and research the companies worth your time. You’ll learn to use company career sites and Handshake the smart way.

    You leave with: a target list of companies and job alerts working for you.

  4. 04

    Session 4

    A Resume That Gets Read

    We rewrite your resume together so it passes applicant tracking systems and impresses a real person. You’ll also learn how to use AI tools the right way in your search, without sounding like a robot.

    You leave with: a polished resume and a cover letter template you can tailor in minutes.

  5. 05

    Session 5

    A Profile Recruiters Can Find

    We build your LinkedIn and Handshake profiles step by step: headline, summary, photo, skills and the keywords recruiters search for.

    You leave with: complete, professional profiles that work for you 24/7.

  6. 06

    Session 6

    Networking and Career Fairs

    Networking is how most jobs get filled. We practice how to start conversations, ask for informational interviews and follow up.

    You leave with: ready-to-send LinkedIn messages, a career fair game plan and an outreach routine.

  7. 07

    Session 7

    Interview Preparation

    We break down how interviews really work and what recruiters listen for. You’ll learn the STAR method, build your best stories and practice answering the tough questions, in person and on video.

    You leave with: your top interview stories ready to go, a virtual interview checklist and thank-you email templates.

  8. 08

    Session 8

    Final Mock Interviews and Program Recap

    Time to put it all together. We run full mock interviews the way real recruiters do, with honest feedback so you walk into the real thing with confidence. Then we recap the most important lessons from the program, review your progress and answer any questions you still have.

    You leave with: real interview practice under your belt, a clear plan for your next steps and the confidence to keep going on your own.

This isn’t goodbye.

When the program ends, our relationship doesn’t. You and I stay in touch. Whether you have a question, need advice before an interview, want a second pair of eyes on an offer or just need some guidance along the way, I’ll be here for you, at no additional cost.

Great preparation doesn’t end with the program. Neither does my support.

Preparation today opens the doors you want tomorrow.

Small, specific steps at your own pace, and a coach in your corner who keeps you on track.
